// 06 ABOUT

About ElectroReck

ElectroReck is the electrical and electronics engineering student community at NIT Kurukshetra. We organise technical workshops, project builds, competitions, seminars and inter-college meets: a place to learn by doing, alongside peers who love circuits, embedded systems, power and everything in between.

Frequently asked questions

Who can join ElectroReck?

Any NIT Kurukshetra student with an interest in electrical and electronics engineering. Create a student account to get started.

Do I need an account to register for events?

No. Event registration is open without an account. A student account lets you track your registrations in one place.

Are events free?

Fees, if any, will be listed on each event. Many community activities are free.

How do I stay updated?

Follow us on Instagram (@ereck_nitkkr) and LinkedIn (erecknitkkr).

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

1. Information we collect

When you register for an event or create a student account, we collect the details you enter: name, roll number, email, phone number, year and branch.

2. How we use it

We use this information only to run ElectroReck events: confirming registrations, contacting participants about schedules or changes, and preparing attendance lists.

3. Storage

On this website, registration and account data is stored in your own browser. Passwords are not stored in plain text. If we move to an online database in future, this policy will be updated.

4. Sharing

We do not sell your data. Information may be shared with the ElectroReck organising team and, where required, NIT Kurukshetra authorities for event administration.

5. Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete your registration details by writing to ereck@nitkkr.ac.in.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy: ereck@nitkkr.ac.in

Terms & Conditions

Last updated: 15 October 2026

1. Eligibility

Events are intended for students. Some events may be restricted to NIT Kurukshetra students or specific years; details are listed on each event.

2. Registration

You must provide correct information. Registration does not guarantee a seat where capacity is limited. The organisers may cancel duplicate or false registrations.

3. Conduct

Participants must follow lab safety rules, instructions of the organisers and institute discipline. Equipment must be handled with care.

4. Changes

Dates, venues and event details may change. Updates will be posted on this website and our social channels.
